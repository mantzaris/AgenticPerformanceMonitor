POST-HOC PAIRED STATISTICAL ANALYSIS

Purpose: Reviewer 2, point 3 (statistical comparison).
Source: artifacts/stage6/reports/per_case.json; 24 paired people, four conditions.
Endpoint: complete valid visible answers, using unchanged frozen judgments.
Reproduce: python3 paper-revised/analysis/paired_statistics.py

The two-sided exact McNemar test conditions on the number of discordant pairs
and uses a Binomial(n_discordant, 0.5) null. We apply Holm adjustment jointly to
all six pairwise comparisons of the four conditions. No continuity correction
or 96-independent-observation approximation is used. This analysis was added
after review and is exploratory, not prespecified confirmatory inference.

VALIDATION

All 96 source rows and 24 distinct task people are checked. Complete counts
match 24/21/15/15 exactly. Known exact p-values and Holm examples are asserted
in the script. The exact-test implementation was also checked against
scipy.stats.binomtest for all 325 possible nonnegative discordance-count pairs
with sum at most 24, agreeing to absolute tolerance 1e-14. The script and output
use no external runtime package, random draw, network request, or model call.
