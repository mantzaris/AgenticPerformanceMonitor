REVIEWER 1 — GRAPHICAL DEMONSTRATION / STUDENT LEARNING
The original activity and reference-switch illustrations are retained. The revised
discussion explicitly distinguishes recorded activity from knowledge acquisition:
no intervention or learning-outcome study was conducted. Graphs cannot honestly
show improvements caused by the dashboard. This is a clarified limitation, NOT
new evidence of better student results; that requested demonstration remains
unavailable.

REVIEWER 2.1 — TWO INDEPENDENT EVALUATORS / UPDATED COMPLETENESS

REVIEWER 2.2 — INDEPENDENT TEST CASES
No new agent runs or reserved-case evaluation were performed. The manuscript says

REVIEWER 2.3 — STATISTICAL COMPARISON
Added post hoc exact two-sided McNemar tests on the 24 paired people, with Holm
adjustment across all six condition pairs. All agent-to-agent adjusted p-values
are at least 0.2813. Under frozen scoring, enumeration versus B and C gives
adjusted p=0.0234; under conditional B17–20 credit, enumeration versus B becomes
p=0.3125. The paper gives effect sizes and limits interpretation because cases
were structurally selected and share course/reference pools. No equivalence or
held-out generalization is claimed. Full calculations: analysis/paired_statistics.*.

REVIEWER 2.4 — AGENT VALUE / AMBIGUOUS QUESTIONS
The discussion now states that no added agent value over enumeration is established
for this structured registry. It identifies ambiguous questions, clarification,
and larger libraries as possible settings for a separately matched comparison.
The discussion request is addressed; suggested ambiguous-question experiments
were not performed.

REVIEWER 2.5 — DUPLICATE-RECORD SENSITIVITY
Added a new calculation using the preserved full development feature bundle with
exact repeated source rows removed, with the same 24 people, profiles, weighting,
thresholds, and 81 exclusions. The independent reducer checks 1,260 primary values
against frozen evidence. Among 52 required feature/reference records, all 29
supported click-rate contrast signs, all 13 supported personal-change directions,
all support statuses, all 16 assessment summaries, and all eight reference-direction
classifications remain unchanged. Numerical magnitudes do change and are reported.
The illustrated A05 contrasts are checked explicitly. Bootstrap intervals were
not recomputed; models and completeness scoring were not rerun. Exact equal rows
need not be errors, so deletion is an alternative treatment, not a correction.
Full calculations: analysis/duplicate_sensitivity.*.
