learning. No study of educational benefit exists, so no such benefit is claimed.

Reviewer 2:
1. Scoring: the four missed B answers and the alternative 19/24 count are explained.

2. Independent cases: reserved students have not been evaluated. This remains a
   limitation, and the added analyses do not claim to be an independent test.

4. Agent value: the rule-based baseline remains preferable for the tested tasks;
