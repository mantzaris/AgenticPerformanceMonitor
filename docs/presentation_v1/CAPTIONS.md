describe new model inference, fresh analysis, a usability study or a new accuracy

(a) Earlier and recent windows are highlighted on a recorded click-rate trajectory,
with a selected personal change and a separately selected recent peer comparison.
(b) Named reference definitions share a quantitative scale; the highlighted saved

Recorded clicks do not measure effort or learning. Intervals describe peer means,
not individuals or differences; the personal change has no uncertainty interval.
No improvement in accuracy or usability is established by this figure.
