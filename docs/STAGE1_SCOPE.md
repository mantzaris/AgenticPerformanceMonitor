# Stage 1 scope, fixed before data analysis

Started 2026-09-18 16:09:04 UTC. Branch: `stage1/research-foundations`.
The supplied research plan is preserved unchanged. No applicable AGENTS.md,
existing code, data, or dependency configuration was present in this repository.

**Question:** Can a bounded investigator request reproducible comparisons of an
individual's recorded platform activity against course peers, a prior-attempt
subgroup, and their own earlier history, while preserving reference definitions,
eligibility and measurement limitations in a validated dashboard?

Build one OULAD adapter, five weekly features, a random-intercept working model,
person-cluster bootstrap peer means, typed evidence/specification contracts, a
deterministic generator, a controlled Altair renderer with self-contained HTML
and PNG exports, and one local instruct-model tool loop. Use two presentations
chosen in lexical order from distinct modules meeting structural criteria:
100 development students with at least eight eligible weeks and at least two
dated non-exam assessments by day 83. Select focal students by a stable hash,
without inspecting their feature values or conclusions. The three questions
concern personal change, reference/stage sensitivity, and measurement limits.

Time origin is course day zero; cutoff is inclusive day 83 (12 seven-day bins).
Earlier baseline is weeks 0–3; recent is 8–11. This is retrospective simulation
of an as-of view, not a validated live deployment. Split every person across all
registrations using a fixed salted SHA-256 rule: 70% development, 30% reserved.
Reserved outcomes and trajectories are not used for examples, fitting or prompts.
Dataset-wide schema/key/missingness auditing is allowed before the split.

Resource ceilings: six elapsed hours, two cumulative hours for our GPU model
processes including startup, 20 attempted generations including failures and
repairs, at most one final-specification repair per question. One model and
backend; new weights under approximately 20 GB. No existing model was found on
the connected pod; prefer Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, BF16, Transformers, existing Torch.
Record GPU process time separately from stage wall time and unknown pod billing.

Acceptance: audited source and keys; two supported presentations; frozen
person splits and cutoff rules; interpretable mixed model or documented failure;
three materially distinct real-data deterministic dashboards; resolvable and
validated evidence; actual GPU generation→tool→result→specification sequence;
attempt all three questions; replay without inference; focused boundary tests;
review entry point, metadata and committed branch. A fallback is not agent success.

Excluded: held-out evaluation, training, model sweeps, causal explanations,
anomaly calibration, broad simulations, a second domain, multi-agent experiments,
manuscript and publishing. The full plan's later-stage interfaces are not promised.

Assumptions requiring review: registration records approximate administrative
eligibility; same-presentation peers and previous-attempt strata answer distinct
descriptive questions; study credits and demographics lack capture timestamps
and are omitted. Scores have no release timestamps and are withheld. Scheduled
assessment opportunities are not proven individual obligations; banked credit
has uncertain availability and cannot establish a prospective exemption. Zero
clicks mean zero recorded activity, never verified logging failure, low effort
or low ability. No novelty, superiority or generalization claim is established.
