# Descriptive analysis protocol v1

The unit is a person–course–presentation–week. Week zero is days 0–6 since presentation start. Profiles in `configs/stage2.json` jointly define complete-week cutoff, earlier personal window and recent window. No tool can extend a profile beyond its cutoff. Weekly state is contemporaneous: a scheduled non-submission in week 3 is not retrospectively changed by a later submission. Scores and banked assessment credit remain unavailable because release/approval time is unobserved. No final results or future activity/withdrawal enter tools or prompts.

For a feature, the focal estimand is the arithmetic mean over that person's administratively eligible, non-null weeks in the requested window. The course reference estimand is the equal-weight mean of peer-specific means over their eligible observed weeks. This is not a ratio of pooled clicks and days. A one-day eligible week and a seven-day eligible week have equal weight in the window mean after forming the within-week rate; this convention can amplify partial-week variation. Counts report distinct people and contributing person-weeks, not registrations disguised as people. At least 20 peers and two focal weeks are required for a displayed focal-minus-peer contrast. A personal recent-minus-earlier change requires at least two observed weeks in each window. Thresholds are engineering support rules, not validated power criteria. Raw descriptive means/counts may still be shown when a contrast is withheld.

The common reference population is development people in the same presentation, excluding all 18 construction/pilot focal people and the original three demonstration people across registrations. Course peers estimate the presentation-wide eligible reference mean. Same-prior-attempt peers restrict to first versus repeat enrollment; this assumes prior attempts were known at entry. Earlier-stage peers use the question's earlier baseline window for the reference summary while keeping the focal requested window. They change the time comparison; they are not automatically fairer, exposure matched, or causally adjusted. Charts always show same-week peer trajectories; the earlier-stage summary is separately labeled. No demographic proxy is introduced.

Zero recorded activity means administratively eligible with no positive recorded clicks; it does not establish zero study or complete logging. Logging completeness is unknown for everyone. Administrative ineligibility produces null feature values, distinct from a zero. Missing registration yields unknown eligibility and unavailable measurements. Nonbanked submission counts and scheduled opportunities without a recorded nonbanked submission are distinct from these states; exemptions and banking approvals are unknown, so scheduled opportunities are not verified personal obligations. Clicks do not measure effort, ability, study time, marks or learning.

Peer composition changes by week because registration, withdrawal and availability change. Trajectory denominators are therefore per week. A window's equal-weight person mean can include different numbers of weeks per person; earlier and recent reference populations can differ. No balanced-panel, missing-at-random or stable-composition assumption is asserted. These comparisons do not identify a causal reason for change and do not calibrate an individual anomaly detector.

The pointwise 95% percentile bootstrap resamples people with replacement, carrying each person's within-window mean (and thus their observed history) together. There are 500 replicates with a deterministic recorded seed. Weekly intervals separately resample the eligible people at that week. These intervals describe uncertainty of a reference mean under empirical-person resampling, conditional on the observed data/eligibility process. They are not individual prediction intervals, not simultaneous bands and not uncertainty for a focal contrast, personal change, or difference between references. No interval is calculated with fewer than two peers. Direction differences across references are descriptive signs, not significance tests; equal signs do not prove invariance of magnitude.

Primary preparation preserves every source interaction row, as in Stage 1. The bounded sensitivity uses exact-row deduplication as a different, unvalidated data interpretation; it does not claim to correct the data. Both estimates and denominators are saved for the six construction examples. No deduplication choice is tuned against pilot outcomes.

The historical AAA random-intercept fit failed with a singular-matrix error under L-BFGS. We inspect design rank, between/within variability and at most one alternative optimizer fit, with a time/iteration cap and recorded warnings. Descriptive tools remain the shared backend regardless of this diagnostic's result. The mixed model is not a validated detector and supplies no dashboard claims.

Independent checking: an independent row-loop reducer and scalar-loop bootstrap verify every bound numerical result against the immutable source bundle. Separate raw-source SQL checks and small hand-calculated fixtures test representative preparation quantities. Neither constitutes independent human review. Rubrics, support rules, task realism and interpretation still require external scientific review.
