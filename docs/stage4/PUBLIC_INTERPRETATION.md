# Shared public interpretation rules v1

These rules and feature definitions are supplied equally to every method. They
are interface semantics, not per-task answer keys or prescribed tool sequences.

- A request about personal change means recent minus earlier within-person means
  using the stated profile. A peer comparison means focal minus the named peer
  mean over the named windows. Bare "peers" means the same course presentation.
- Recorded click rate is `clicks_per_eligible_day`: first clicks per eligible day
  within a week, then equal-weight observed-week means. Active days and distinct
  resources are different measurements. New questions name the requested feature.
- `nonbanked_submissions` is the weekly count of recorded nonbanked submissions;
  `scheduled_no_submission` is scheduled non-exam assessment opportunities without
  a recorded nonbanked submission at that week's end. Report observed-week means,
  not claims about verified obligations or eventual failure.
- Each analysis returns its named peer contrast AND the personal earlier/recent
  summaries. A separate baseline-window request changes the peer contrast window;
  it is unnecessary merely to obtain personal history.
- A request to compare reference definitions does not request personal history
  unless the wording says so. Earlier-stage peers answer another descriptive
  question; they are not automatically fairer or causally adjusted.
- A requested comparison can be answered with a scoped supported estimate or
  scoped insufficiency. Identify the feature, windows and reference involved.
  A general insufficiency statement cannot excuse other unanswered requests.
- Zero recorded activity, administrative ineligibility, unknown eligibility and
  unknown logging completeness differ. Scores and banking approval times are
  unavailable; clicks/submission records do not establish marks or learning.
- Use distinct people and contributing weeks, and label intervals as pointwise
  peer-mean bootstrap intervals. Personal change and focal contrasts have no
  uncertainty interval. All conclusions are descriptive and noncausal.
- Display relevant answers, not merely retrieve them. Extra analyses or repeated
  panels are optional and do not compensate for missing requested information.
  Controlled wording, values and standard caveats are supplied by the compiler;
  the method selects analyses and answers in its assigned interface. Required structure/context has separate provenance.
