# Presentation v2: saved content and responsibility

Starting commit: `dd4023f01e8f717cf005f7394e33ac35ab34cf12`, clean `main`.
The inventory in `artifacts/presentation_v2/historical_inventory.json` records all
7,799 previously tracked files. Presentation v1, pre-draft v1, manuscript draft v1,
and all frozen experiments remain available and unchanged.

This is a small presentation extension. It imports the unchanged presentation-v1
selected-evidence mapper and pre-draft renderer/chart functions. It does not load
rubrics, unselected tool results, agents, models or a statistical dispatcher. It
adds responsive typography/hierarchy, a visible historical output-status line, a
responsibility expander, recent selected peer emphasis in the personal view, and
bookmarkable saved-reference state. The original question, conclusion, claims,
panels, caveats, IDs and evidence remain inspectable. Layout selection continues
to depend on claim kinds, feature families, references and support states, not
case IDs. The inherited fixed example list is only an input selection manifest.

## Preservation, duplicate content and excerpts

The inherited loader restricts cards and controls to claim-selected evidence.
An evidence record used for personal change does not authorize its peer summary
as an additional peer answer. The s6_15 agent's two earlier peer answers remain
weeks 0–3, while its separate personal-insufficiency answer remains weeks 10–11.
The omitted recent-peer answer is not inserted. The baseline's explicit recent-peer
insufficiency is shown separately. S1's annotations are editorial, outside captures,
and use the existing Stage 6 trace audit. No evaluator or success-rate calculation runs.

Only exactly equivalent personal claims are consolidated: original text, origin,
feature/unit, focal context, windows, means, counts and support must agree. The
peer definition is irrelevant to personal identity. Personal cases A and E each
retain both original indices/evidence IDs for the duplicate. Distinct peer identities
or bootstrap endpoints are never merged. Every original panel remains accessible.

Expanded views expose all distinct selected answers; personal-view peer cards are
ordered recent first. Publication excerpts are labeled excerpts, not complete-answer
certificates. Personal excerpts emphasize the selected recent peer answer; its
selected earlier peer answer stays in the expanded view. Observation excerpts keep
availability and the compiler timeline, with dated states in the expanded view.
Reference excerpts show the selected saved reference; all three choices remain
available. Assessment excerpts keep measures and dated states; the original trajectory
remains expanded. S1 omits repeated headers/trajectory, while retaining every selected
peer claim and the personal-insufficiency answer. Original complete dashboards,
not excerpts, remain the basis for scientific review.

## Measurement meaning

- A displayed difference is formatted from its unrounded saved value. Thus 14.07
  and 3.54 accompany −10.54; no rounded-number subtraction replaces the calculation.
- Peer means weight eligible people equally after within-person weekly averaging.
  Counts distinguish people, person-weeks and focal observed weeks. The focal person
  and all task focal people were excluded from the saved reference pool.
- Same-prior-attempt selection changes population; earlier-period selection changes
  reference time and potentially eligible membership. It is not causal adjustment
  or a claim of a fairer population.
- The original 95% person-bootstrap intervals concern peer means. Trajectory bands
  are pointwise. They are not personal prediction intervals, contrast intervals or
  uncertainty for personal change. No interval is created here.
- Recorded zero, ineligibility, unknown administrative state and unavailable values
  are distinct. Logging completeness is unknown separately. Nulls break lines.
  Observation status means click-record state even for an assessment feature; it
  is not inferred from that feature's value. The unknown-state legend does not
  imply that unknown periods were injected into these real examples.
- Assessment means count non-banked submissions or scheduled assessments without a
  recorded submission per observed week. Dated states retain their as-of dates;
  a day-55 snapshot is not relabeled a day-83 status. Grades and banking approval
  are unavailable without release/approval timing, not because of sample size.
  These records do not establish effort, learning, ability or achievement.

## Saved-reference interaction

The selector updates the chart, highlighted result, units/windows, counts and
evidence link together. A query parameter records the exact saved evidence ID;
its permalink restores that state. An unknown ID hides the result and chart,
shows an explicit unavailable message and requires choosing an available record.
There is no nearest-reference substitution, new analytical call, network request
or model invocation. Figure 2 fixes the focal value (3.50), weeks (8–11), unit and
0–8 scale, changing reference weeks 8–11 to 0–3. Counts change from 1,024 to 1,128.

Source-hash checks and historical replay establish saved numerical binding, not
question completeness or independent scientific review. The frozen Stage 6 scoring
caveat and pending human review remain unchanged. This package demonstrates
implemented presentation behavior, without new accuracy or usability evidence.
