# Stage 3 analysis protocol v1

The descriptive backend, five features, three reference definitions, profiles,
500-replicate person bootstrap, support thresholds (20 peers and two focal weeks),
primary source-row treatment and independent numerical validator remain exactly
those in [Stage 2](../stage2/ANALYSIS_PROTOCOL.md). The mixed model remains a
separate diagnostic artifact and supplies no dashboard claim. No new fit or
duplicate-handling experiment is needed for this policy/evaluation intervention.

Each mean weights observed eligible weeks equally within person, then people
equally for peers. Reference pools exclude all prior focal people and the twelve
new Stage 3 focal people across registrations. Changing eligibility and unequal
histories can change peer composition; partial weeks retain equal window weight
after forming their daily rates. Earlier-stage peers change the question rather
than causally adjust it. No missing-at-random or complete-logging assumption is
made. Bootstrap intervals describe pointwise reference means conditional on the
observed records, not individual prediction or contrast uncertainty.

Prospective information stops at each complete-week profile cutoff. No future
clicks, final results, future withdrawals or scores of unknown release time enter
tools or model context. A scheduled assessment without a contemporaneously
recorded nonbanked submission is not proof of failure or an obligation. Banked
approval time is unknown. All records are reused unmodified from the verified
development-only source bundle. Selection uses administrative structure only.

Evaluator facts use the independent row-loop reducer, not the production groupby
path; this is computational cross-checking, not independent human review.
