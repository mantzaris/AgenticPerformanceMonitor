# Focused contribution framing before inference

Natural-language visualization and structured analytical representations are
established. [NL4DV](https://nl4dv.github.io/nl4dv/) maps a dataset and natural
language query to attributes, analytical tasks and Vega-Lite specifications.
[Sah et al. (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.13391) investigate LLM-generated
analytic specifications, including data attributes, tasks and visualization
designs. A compact semantic representation is therefore not itself a novelty
claim for this project.

[Draco (Moritz et al., 2018)](https://www.domoritz.de/papers/2018-Draco-InfoVis.pdf)
represents visualization design knowledge with logical constraints and supports
design reasoning. Deterministic layout compilation and constraint checking are
established ideas. This experiment instead measures whether explicitly selecting
a particular longitudinal comparison helps requested answers reach a display;
it does not establish a new visualization constraint system.

[W3C PROV-DM](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/) describes provenance through entities,
activities, agents and derivations. Recording evidence sources and compiler
responsibility applies established provenance principles. Tool use, JSON
schemas, reproducible calculations and deterministic rendering are engineering
features, not sufficient claims of a new scientific contribution.

The candidate contribution is a controlled development study of responsibility
allocation in evidence-grounded dashboards: separate support/scope derivation
from explicit answer identity, retain genuine model selection responsibility,
and account separately for visible answers and model-selected answers. Whether
the results support a useful binding effect, only a metadata/interface effect,
or neither must be decided from the frozen ablation. B versus A also adds the
shared returned-answer catalogue, so it estimates that documented interface
package, not a pure causal effect of deleting redundant fields.

External review must assess the evaluator's interpretation of user questions,
scientific appropriateness of comparison populations and support rules, and
whether this bounded failure analysis adds enough beyond prior work. No novelty,
general reliability, causal educational inference, human usability, or advantage
over unrestricted natural-language systems is established in this stage.
