# Contribution assessment after the frozen ablation

**Supported empirical finding.** On these 24 fresh development people, explicit
identity selection did not improve the frozen completeness endpoint: C 15/24 versus
A 21/24 and B 15/24; method-selected completeness was 11/24, 18/24 and 14/24,
respectively. Enumeration answered 24/24 with no model calls. B improved accepted
specification validity to 24/24, but validity alone did not establish completeness.
All accepted quantitative outputs passed source-bound validation. No agent met the
combined gate, including separate personal/peer insufficiency on all four cases.
These statements concern one checkpoint, fixed greedy decoding, known question
families and one attempt per case—not agents generally or an independent population
confirmation. Earlier stages are not pooled into this sample.

**Measurement limitation.** The frozen scorer under-credits eight focal mean items
in four B assessment pages: the means appear explicitly in personal-change claims.
The [display/scoring caveat](SCORING_CAVEAT.md) preserves primary scores and the
actual text. A conditional four-case correction would still leave B below the
engineering gate. Human adjudication is pending. The study cannot claim a fully
validated completeness measure or publication readiness.

**Candidate methodological contribution.** The defensible direction is an audited
study of reliability and division of responsibility: distinguish numerical
integrity, selected analytical meaning, visible completeness and compiler context;
compare metadata derivation with identity selection under equivalent returned
information; retain negative cases and answer provenance. Exact identities and
checked personal-summary equivalence provide an inspectable implementation, but
this experiment does not show that they improve answer selection. Mechanically
valid bindings can omit a requested answer or select the wrong focal window.

**Established engineering.** Natural-language analytic task specifications and
visualization generation are established in [NL4DV](https://nl4dv.github.io/nl4dv/)
and [Sah et al.'s LLM analytic-specification work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.13391).
Logical visualization constraints are established in
[Draco](https://www.domoritz.de/papers/2018-Draco-InfoVis.pdf). Entity/activity/agent
provenance and derivation are established in [PROV-DM](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/).
This work applies those ideas to answer selection in longitudinal comparisons;
JSON, tool use, deterministic rendering and provenance are not novelty claims.
The focused [prior-art assessment](PRIOR_ART.md) gives the precise relationship.

**Unsupported claims.** No general agent incapability, novel statistical detector,
causal educational explanation, broad natural-language understanding, human
usability benefit, repeatability, held-out reliability or superiority over
enumeration has been established. The baseline operates over a small predefined
question/reference registry; its success does not establish unrestricted language
understanding. Nor would passing the engineering gate alone establish novelty.

**Direction.** Retain enumeration for this prototype. Between the new interventions,
B is simpler, has higher validity and method-selected completeness, and costs less;
there is no measured reason to prefer C. Neither replaces A as the strongest
observed agent control, and none replaces enumeration. End further prompt/policy
or binding-interface tuning on this same registry. The next bounded work should
be external scientific/rubric review of the existing packet with no new inference
or reserved data, followed only by a separately authorized, versioned scoring
audit. Decide then whether the transparent negative reliability study supports a
contribution or whether a materially different research question is needed.
Do not automatically launch another experiment or write a manuscript.
