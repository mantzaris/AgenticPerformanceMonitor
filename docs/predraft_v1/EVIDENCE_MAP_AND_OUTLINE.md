# Pre-draft evidence map and outline — not a manuscript

The defensible question is how to divide responsibility for selecting, calculating,
checking and presenting longitudinal analytical answers. Current evidence supports
an audited development study of completeness failures, including negative ablation
results. It does not support unrestricted frontend generation, an accuracy or
usability gain from the new presentation, superiority over enumeration, or transfer
to other domains.

## Outline and readiness of sections

1. **Research question and contribution.** Frame reliability of evidence-grounded
   analytical content selection and responsibility accounting. A negative result
   can inform this question. Existing artifacts support a candidate argument;
   novelty and a defensible completeness construct still require review.
2. **Related-work questions to verify before drafting.** Begin with the focused
   primary-source set in [Stage 6 prior art](../stage6/PRIOR_ART.md): NL4DV, structured
   LLM analytic specifications, Draco and W3C PROV. Check precisely what each
   already establishes about task representation, selection versus rendering,
   constraints, and provenance. Check whether closest systems evaluate requested
   answer completeness separately from validity and layout. Do not claim novelty
   from tools, JSON, semantic schemas or deterministic charts. No new broad survey
   was performed in this preparation; prior-art claims need final primary-source
   verification, including any later work, before writing them as contributions.
3. **Data and measurements.** Existing OULAD audit, Stage 2 analysis protocol,
   cutoffs, development/reserved person split, recorded-click and assessment
   definitions, duplicate handling, equal-person weighting and support rules can
   supply this section. Scientific judgments on support thresholds, changing peer
   composition, unequal histories and reference appropriateness remain open.
   Grade release timing is unknown; absent clicks do not prove logging failure.
4. **System design and responsibility boundaries.** Existing code and Figure 3
   support a precise account of deterministic computation, generic tool iteration,
   A/B/C selection interfaces, validation, compiler context and controlled
   rendering. Describe the new presentation as post-experiment rendering of saved
   cases. The saved selector does not rerun analyses.
5. **Experimental design.** Draftable from the frozen Stage 6 protocol: 24 new
   development people, four per known family, paired four-condition design, fixed
   reference pool, exclusions, budgets, decoding, counterbalancing and failures.
   Earlier stages are development history, not a continuous improvement series
   or an independent confirmation sample.
6. **Results and failure analysis.** Frozen counts and costs are reproducible now.
   Figure 4 must carry the B17–20 caveat. Equivalent forms (also C21), scoped
   insufficiency and unavailable-quantity judgments need independent review before
   treating coverage as a scientifically settled endpoint. Any revised analysis
   must be separately versioned and explicitly post hoc, alongside original scores.
7. **Limitations.** Small development sample, one domain/two course offerings,
   limited task/reference vocabulary, one checkpoint and one attempt, dependent
   methods per person, evolving earlier development, provisional rubric, no user
   study and unopened evaluation population. Presentation polish is not usability
   evidence. The engineering threshold is an internal decision rule, not a
   conference acceptance requirement; missing it is not automatic unpublishability.
8. **Remaining validation.** First complete the two independent reviews and
   adjudication. Decide whether a bounded reliability/negative-result contribution
   is sufficient or whether a different question is needed. A separately authorized
   fixed validation study may then be proposed using the unopened person-level
   population. The [existing conditional draft](../stage6/FINAL_VALIDATION_DRAFT.md)
   remains a proposal, not permission to execute it. No fixed agent has been
   selected for final validation here.

## Decisions before drafting or validation

- Adjudicate B17–20, separately displayed personal windows including C21, unavailable
  answer forms, and separate personal/peer insufficiency. Review justified
  equivalent answers rather than requiring one template.
- Review the scientific estimands, duplicated-source treatment, support thresholds,
  reference suitability and interval interpretation. Source equality alone cannot
  settle these choices.
- Decide the claim's scope: audited development reliability study, operational
  enumeration case study, or a different question. Do not make a positive agent
  superiority claim from these artifacts.
- Verify related work and choose final main/supplementary figures after review.
  Figures 1–4 and S1–S2 are candidates; the proof is not a manuscript.
- If further validation is justified, preregister one fixed system/claim, scorer,
  outcomes, person-level sampling, uncertainty and resource ceilings before opening
  any reserved observations. This preparation authorizes none of those executions.
