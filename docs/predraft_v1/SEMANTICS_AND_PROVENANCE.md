## Measurement wording

- “Presentation peers” means other development students in the same course offering,
  excluding the focal person and all task focal people. Exact saved definitions and
  counts remain in the inspector. Prior-attempt selection changes population;
  earlier-period selection changes time and may also change eligible membership.
- Recorded clicks are not effort, ability, time spent or learning. Zero recorded
  clicks, administrative ineligibility, unknown logging completeness and unavailable
  measurements are distinct. Assessment row `observation_status` denotes click
  state even when its feature value is an assessment count.
- Personal means 14.07 and 3.54 and the change −10.54 come from unrounded saved
  quantities; subtraction of rounded display values would give −10.53. The new
  wording explains this; it does not change a calculation.
- Peer intervals are saved 95% person-bootstrap intervals for the peer mean.
  They are not individual prediction intervals, contrast intervals, or personal
  change intervals. “Uncertainty for the change was not estimated” replaces the
  old terse wording in these new views only.
- Assessment counts are observed-week means. Dated states retain their original
  `as_of_day`; a later submission does not rewrite an earlier snapshot. Unknown
  grade release/banked approval timing is not a sample-size failure.
