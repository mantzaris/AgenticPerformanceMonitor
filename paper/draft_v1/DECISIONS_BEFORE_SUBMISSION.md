This is a complete author-review draft, not a submission-readiness determination.
Do not alter the frozen scores to settle these questions. No reviewer was contacted
or assigned a judgment during drafting.

1. **Answer equivalence.** Have two independent reviewers assess all 96 original
   method/case slots using the [existing packet](../../artifacts/predraft_v1/human_review/index.html)
   and reconcile disagreements. In particular: B17–20 display requested recent
   assessment means within personal-change claims; C21/C23 display earlier/recent
   means without an explicit personal contrast; unavailable-quantity forms and
   separate personal/peer insufficiency need consistent interpretation. Any new
   analysis must retain the original scores and be versioned and labeled post hoc.
   Publication excerpts must not replace full original dashboards for this review.
2. **Measurement and references.** Review summing repeated interaction rows,
   partial-week and equal-person weighting, the two-focal-week/20-peer support
   rules, changing eligible membership, prior-attempt grouping, and the earlier
   reference period. Decide whether the descriptive interpretations are defensible.
   Grade and banked-approval timing remain unavailable. Source equality cannot
   settle these scientific judgments.
3. **Contribution.** The present argument is a bounded study of responsibility and
   completeness, including a negative interface ablation. Structured specifications,
   omissions, tool use, provenance, and constrained rendering already exist in prior
   work. Decide whether the longitudinal scope/provenance accounting and inspected
   failures add a sufficient empirical contribution. Do not claim enumeration
   superiority outside this registry, improved usability, or general reliability.
4. **Validation claim.** A narrowly framed development report can be drafted from
   existing evidence. A stronger reliability claim needs a separately approved,
   fixed person-level study after adjudication, with enumeration and appropriate
   A/B controls, frozen outcomes and grouped uncertainty. No agent currently passes
   the combined engineering gate. That gate is not a conference acceptance rule.
   The [conditional protocol](../../docs/stage6/FINAL_VALIDATION_DRAFT.md) is a proposal,
   not authorization to open reserved observations or run inference.
5. **Regular-paper scope.** ICAART describes regular papers as completed and
   validated research. Authors must decide whether the finalized, adjudicated scope
   justifies this track and whether additional validation is needed. This draft
   targets the requested regular-paper format without asserting that unresolved
   scientific review is complete.
6. **AI disclosure placement.** Guidelines request an acknowledgments disclosure
   but also omission of acknowledgments for anonymous review. The AI-tools page
   additionally permits an appropriate disclosure section. Draft V1 uses a
   nonidentifying Section 9 plus an abstract citation and a citation footnote in
   each substantive section. Confirm final placement and wording; do not silently
   remove disclosure. It names Codex assistance with prose, code, references and
   figures, distinguishes experimental Qwen, and claims no completed human review.
7. **Anonymity, source release, and supplementary material.** Author information
   and identifying repository URLs are absent from the PDF. Internal review notes
   intentionally retain repository paths and must be kept separate from an anonymous
   submission package. Confirm public-repository/preprint arrangements before any
   submission: the venue restricts public posting while under review. No explicit
   regular-paper supplementary-material permission was found; the main PDF is
   scientifically self-contained and does not rely on an accepted supplement.
   The unused assessment figure remains an author-review candidate only.
8. **Final editorial checks.** Authors must verify every statement and citation,
   the final venue character count, any disclosure update, and the final anonymous
   PDF. The 12-page working length does not replace the character rule. Retain the
   B17–20 caveat in the abstract, results caption, and results discussion if primary
